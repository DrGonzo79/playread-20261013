# Office Hours — PlayRead

**Mode:** startup diagnostic. **Status:** evidence-limited design decision.

## Six forcing answers

1. **Demand reality:** a named incumbent sells 4–8 week risk assessments to named publishers, and publishers already pay consultants and lawyers. That proves spend around diligence, not demand for PlayRead. The next proof is one publisher sharing a build under NDA and paying for a standardized memo.
2. **Status quo:** a scout filters hundreds of submissions, asks for successive builds, calls an internal technical producer, commissions separate legal review, and makes a greenlight from fragmented notes. The cost is weeks of delay, expert time, missed risks, and a bad $1M bet.
3. **Desperate customer:** the head of scouting or portfolio director at a 10–50 person indie publisher signing 10–30 titles annually. Their reputation and slate economics suffer when a promising demo hides code debt, unclear asset rights, or an impossible production plan.
4. **Narrowest paid wedge:** a human-led, seven-day greenlight memo for one Unity/Unreal prototype with five evidence-backed risk dimensions and a `SIGN`, `INVESTIGATE`, or `REJECT` recommendation. No continuous platform or autonomous AI verdict yet.
5. **Observation plan:** recruit five publisher scouts through warm introductions; watch each triage the same anonymized build without guidance; record evidence requested, confidence changes, objections to sharing, and whether they pay $4,500 or accept a paid pilot.
6. **Future-fit:** submission volume and generated assets increase, making provenance and build quality harder to infer from polish. PlayRead becomes more essential only if it owns trusted evidence lineage and outcome benchmarks; generic AI detection becomes less useful.

## Premise challenge

- The product is **decision evidence for a high-stakes greenlight**, not an automated code score.
- Third-party build trust is the acquisition constraint; more features do not solve it.
- Doing nothing remains rational for publishers signing only a few games per year. Qualification is mandatory.

## Approaches considered

1. **Concierge memo (recommended, S / medium risk):** analyst-led secure review with a standardized evidence pack. Fastest route to payment and learning.
2. **Read-only risk snapshot (M / medium risk):** local/ephemeral scanner plus human review. Better demoability, but security claims must be proven.
3. **Continuous portfolio diligence platform (XL / high risk):** repository, build, milestone, and benchmark system. Ideal architecture after 50+ completed memos; premature now.

## Success criteria and assignment

Within 30 days: 10 qualified conversations, three observed build triages, two NDA-approved prototype shares, and at least one paid memo at $4,500 or a paid pilot at $2,000+. **Assignment:** ask one known publisher scout to provide an anonymized borderline build and sign a paid pilot letter before building live ingestion.
